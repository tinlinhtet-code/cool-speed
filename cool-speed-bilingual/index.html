WHAT IS PU FOAM?

What is Polyurethane Foam?

PU foam is a spray-applied insulation material that expands and adheres to suitable surfaces, creating a continuous layer that helps reduce heat transfer and air gaps. It provides excellent thermal insulation by reducing heat transfer and helping maintain comfortable indoor temperatures.

Technical Specifications & Thermal Performance

Closed-cell polyurethane (PU) foam is a high-performance insulation material designed to minimize heat transfer, improve energy efficiency, and provide long-lasting thermal protection. Its dense, closed-cell structure offers excellent thermal insulation and helps resist moisture absorption. Key technical properties, including foam density and thermal conductivity (K-Factor), determine its insulation performance and suitability for different applications.

COOL SPEED offers Spray Foam Insulation for accessible surfaces and Injection Foam Insulation for suitable concealed spaces.
